# M3 motion and timestamp model

M3 keeps the historical schema 1.0 synchronized experiments and schema 2.0 sweeps unchanged. New experiment/run envelopes use schema 3.0. Four fixed identified beacons share a positive height; the tag is planar. Known-map and joint-map fits retain the M1 gauge and range-noise objective.

## Motion and acquisition

Paths retain the M2 arclength conventions. `motion.profile` is constant, variable, or stationary; `speed_scale` is 0–10. The variable preset integrates linear signed-speed transitions at duration fractions 0, .15, .35, .45, .55, .7, .8, .9, 1 with relative speeds .3, 1.5, 1.5, 0, 0, 1.8, 0, -.9, -.9. The saved profile is normalized by peak forward excursion. It includes acceleration, a stop, and reversal. The speed chart displays absolute speed; signed speed and along-path acceleration remain in truth.motion. Corners can change spatial direction abruptly; acceleration here is along-path, not a model of vehicle dynamics. Positions hold at endpoints and outside the configured duration. Increasing speed can reach an endpoint earlier; this is disclosed in saved metadata.

Acquisition cycles start at c_i=i/cycle_rate_hz through the duration. A beacon in order slot j is physically acquired at c_i+j*spacing_s+uniform jitter. Ranges use the truth at that actual time. Supplied timestamps add a configured offset and independent uniform timestamp jitter. Delivery time adds latency to the actual time. Independent per-cycle/beacon range-noise draws are preserved across timing and missingness changes. Missing probability removes records; integer beacon strides retain only every kth cycle for that beacon. Schedules may overlap cycles when spacing is large; saved actual times remain authoritative. Playback follows retained cycle/beacon record order, rather than sorting delivery order.

`truth.ping_positions`, `truth.grouped_positions`, and `truth.ping_times_s` align one-for-one with retained observations. `estimator_input.times_s` contains cycle starts, while truth.times_s is the separate display/evaluation grid. Each observation retains epoch, beacon ID, actual time, supplied time, delivery time, range and assumed sigma. Synthetic truth is used only by generation, disclosed truth-near initialization, surveyed-map input, and evaluation; the measurement fitter does not consume it.

## Trajectory, residuals, and optional prior

The estimated planar trajectory has K controlled knots with xy coordinates. Between knots p(t) is linear interpolation. Knot spacing 0 selects the cycle grid plus domain endpoints; positive spacing selects a uniform grid from the domain start, including the final endpoint. The domain covers cycle starts, display times and supplied ping timestamps equally for both timing models. Actual and delivery timestamps do not choose estimator knots. Both models therefore have compatible parameters and initial guesses. No extrapolation enters residuals.

The aware residual is `(norm(p(assumed_time_s)-b_j)-range_m)/sigma_m`. The grouped residual replaces that supplied ping time by `estimator_input.times_s[epoch]`: every cycle uses its start as the common reference. Both fit identical observations. They do not add an unconstrained position for each individual ping. Joint fitting has 2K+6 parameters after gauge fixing; known-map fitting has 2K. Analytic range derivatives distribute position derivatives between the two neighboring knots by their interpolation weights. Optimization uses the M1 bounded TRF baseline, linear loss, normalized unsquared range residuals, analytic Jacobians, and 1e-12 termination tolerances.

`prior_sigma_m=0` disables priors. A positive sigma adds each interior knot minus the time-weighted interpolation of its neighbors, separately in x and y, divided by sigma in metres. This favors locally constant velocity. It is a soft modeling assumption; its effective behavior changes with knot spacing. It is not measured acceleration or odometry. Measurement and prior residuals/objectives, measurement-only and augmented Jacobian spectra/rank, and prior count are separately saved. A stronger prior means smaller sigma. Priors can bias stops and corners and cannot make a rank-deficient measurement configuration observable.

Rank uses the M1 relative threshold 1e-8 with a common metre scale from median absolute range (at least 1 m). Full rank is local and does not prove global uniqueness. Display geometry errors and per-ping model-time errors are separate. Truth sampled on estimator knots and interpolated back provides a representation discrepancy, despite the historical field name `truth_interpolation_floor`; it is not a mathematical lower bound on optimized error. Its actual-time evaluation uses disclosed endpoint holds when clock error leaves truth times outside the estimator domain. The actual-versus-cycle truth gap diagnoses the timing approximation independently of fitted interpolation/prior effects. Each fitted run saves its knot times, knots, display positions, model-time ping positions and supplied-time positions.

## Limits and local application

Duration is .01–3600 s; display samples 8–100; cycle count 2–100; knots at most 150. Noise is 0–100 m and assumed sigma 1e-6–1000 m. The numerical API permits 20 starts and 100000 evaluations; HTTP application submissions permit 12 starts and 5000 evaluations per start. Sweeps permit 40 runs and 240 starts. Invalid settings are rejected explicitly. Fully missing observations produce inspectable failed starts, even with a prior enabled. Coarse grids, unsupported high cycle counts or clock-expanded dense grids are not silently substituted.

Use the existing path/map/noise/seed controls for the M3 base experiment, then the M3 motion/acquisition/model controls and explicit Run M3 or Run paired sweep action. Scrubbing overlays actual tag position, receiving beacon, measured range ray, common cycle truth position and fitted model-time position. It preserves the main camera and leaves saved-comparison settings intact. The speed chart is from saved truth motion, not a fitted velocity estimate. Saved run/sweep import, comparison, JSON/CSV and PNG exports do not solve. Complete artifacts remain in runs/ and runs/artifacts/; interrupted checkpoints disclose that unattempted cells were not solved. The latest successful bundled M3 acceptance directory supplies startup examples once, without solving.

## Timing sweeps and retained numerical evidence

`uwb/m3_sweeps.py` runs bounded one-factor comparisons of the timestamp-aware and grouped estimators from `uwb/m3.py`. The grouped rule assigns all retained pings in a cycle to that cycle's reference timestamp. Actual acquisition time, assumed timestamp and delivery time have separate meanings: acquisition time generates the range; assumed time enters the timestamp-aware objective; delivery time is metadata for the batch estimator.

## Sweep controls and pairing

`validate_sweep_config` accepts `base_experiment`, `options`, `factor`, `values` and `seeds`. It normalizes every cell before solving. The factors are `speed_scale`, `spacing_s`, `knot_spacing_s`, `timestamp_offset_s`, `timestamp_jitter_s`, `missing_probability` and `beacon_every_cycles`. The cadence factor changes only beacon 3's integer cycle stride, leaving beacons 0–2 at every cycle. Each sweep permits 1–10 distinct finite factor values, 1–10 distinct unsigned 32-bit seeds, at most 40 runs and 240 total starts; numerical-module bounds apply to each effective cell.

Each value/seed cell generates one experiment. The two models receive exactly that retained experiment. The grouped model reuses every timestamp-aware initial knot trajectory and beacon geometry. Both models use the same knot grid, including assumed timestamp coverage. Pairing applies within a cell; changing the factor can change the observations or parameterization. The effective initialization seed is `(options.seed + measurement_seed) % 2**32`, retained in run options. A truth-near initialization explicitly uses synthetic truth and supplies diagnostic evidence, not truth-independent recovery evidence.

`run_sweep(config, on_progress=None)` retains every attempted run and start, including exceptions and nonconverged estimates. Each progress snapshot contains all completed attempts. The version `3.0`, `timing_sweep` envelope contains the normalized controls, complete run envelopes, a per-start table and per-value/model aggregates. An interrupted/running snapshot is a disclosed prefix of the deterministic attempt ordering; it is never silently completed during loading.

## Separate quantities

The table reports optimizer convergence, geometric accuracy, range residuals, measurement-only Jacobian rank, augmented rank, measurement and prior objectives, truth-knot interpolation error and the grouped timing approximation separately. Full measurement rank is a local diagnostic and does not establish global uniqueness; prior-augmented rank is not additional measurement information. Geometry distributions include all finite estimates, including nonconverged starts. Selected-start distributions are separate. Convergence-rate denominators include every attempted start, including failures with no estimate.

The disclosed geometry threshold is maximum tag and beacon error at most 1e-5 m for noiseless data and 0.25 m for noisy data. Exact data use raw canonical/surveyed coordinates; noisy joint fits use the saved proper planar rigid map alignment, with scale one and reflections forbidden; known-map fits stay surveyed. These are descriptive thresholds. With asynchronous sampling or a coarse knot grid, interpolation mismatch can prevent exact geometry even on noiseless data. The normalized RMS range-residual threshold is 2, reported independently of geometry and convergence.

`validate_artifact`, `save_artifact`, `load_artifact` and `table_csv` validate retained runs, identity, pairing and accounting, and recompute derived tables without generating observations or solving. CSV contains one row for every attempted start. Existing M1/M2 artifact readers remain independent.

## Reproducible acceptance and benchmarks

Run `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/validate_m3.py` for seven factor sweeps, a zero-spacing comparison against the synchronized M1 estimator using common initial geometry, a stationary-tag fixture, and delivery-latency invariants for both estimators. Run `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/benchmark_m3.py` for paired batch wall-clock measurements at 16 and 32 display samples with all per-start outcomes retained. Every invocation creates a unique directory under `evidence/m3/`; neither script overwrites prior evidence or user runs. The acceptance driver checks fingerprints of pre-existing evidence outside M3 and all pre-existing user runs.

Recorded acceptance results and performance claims must be read from the generated evidence rather than inferred from the configuration. The acceptance sweeps use an explicitly surveyed synthetic map and truth-near starts to isolate timing/interpolation; they do not prove recovery of an unknown map. Joint-map results require their own reported starts, residuals and measurement ranks.

## Measured results (October 13, 2026)

The completed record is [acceptance summary](../../evidence/m3/acceptance_20261013_221140_761f23ec/acceptance_summary.json), with seven full sweeps containing 68 runs and 136 starts, five demo runs and four isolated acquisition/prior examples. Every sweep start converged; that does not imply exact geometry. The forced one-evaluation demo retains both nonconverged estimates. The driver checked 190 pre-existing evidence/history files and found every file unchanged.

Zero spacing reproduced the M1 synchronized ranges and fitted tag/beacon estimates using common initial geometry (estimate tolerance 1e-8 m). A stationary tag recovered to less than 1e-5 m display RMSE in both models with nonzero spacing. Adding 2 s delivery latency preserved fitted tag and beacon coordinates within 1e-12 m in both models.

For the variable-speed, known-map, noiseless rectangle fixture, selected-start median display-position RMSE across seeds was:

| Control | Timestamp-aware | Grouped |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Spacing 0 s | 7.56e-16 m | 7.56e-16 m |
| Spacing 0.04 s | 0.0854 m | 0.271 m |
| Spacing 0.10 s | 0.180 m | 0.661 m |
| Timestamp offset 0.10 s, spacing 0.04 s | 0.478 m | 0.271 m |
| Timestamp jitter amplitude 0.05 s, spacing 0.04 s | 0.133 m | 0.271 m |

At 0.04 s spacing, the actual-versus-cycle truth position gap was 0.312 m RMS, while the true-knot interpolation representation discrepancy at actual pings was 0.101 m RMS. Thus the aware fit's remaining error is disclosed independently of the grouped timing gap. At 1 s knots, the interpolation representation discrepancy rose to 0.709 m RMS; the aware fitted display RMSE was 0.586 m. Knot spacing changes the trajectory representation and information per parameter, so reduced range cost alone is not evidence of better geometry.

In the isolated prior fixture, prior sigma 0.1 m increased measurement objective from 29.04 to 124.84 and supplied prior objective 577.47; display RMSE changed from 0.0854 to 0.150 m. Measurement and augmented ranks were both 34 here. These are measured effects of this particular smoothness assumption and path, not a claim that a prior improves recovery. Isolated acquisition jitter amplitude 0.03 s changed display RMSE from 0.0854 to 0.0957 m with correct assumed timestamps; it is distinct from timestamp jitter.

[Benchmark records](../../evidence/m3/benchmark_20261013_221207_a4550df5/performance_benchmark.json) retain every attempt for four paired runs, each with two starts per model, known map, 0.05 m noise and 0.05 s spacing. Whole paired-sweep wrapper wall times were 0.193/0.193 s at 16 display samples and 0.393/0.385 s at 32 samples on this Windows virtual-environment run. They include generation, solving and aggregation and are not promises of UI latency.

Independent mathematical probes verified the combined analytic Jacobian by a directional central difference (maximum absolute discrepancy 2.35e-8), exact concatenation of measurement and prior residuals, invariance of independent joint fitting to changed synthetic truth geometry, and two retained failures when every range is missing even with a prior enabled. The separate sweep tests also verify pairing, saved-table reuse without solving, tamper detection, bounded controls and complete failure checkpoints.
